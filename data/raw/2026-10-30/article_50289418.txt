Back to Blog Share this post Copy url to clipboard Copied Thinking Summary Mistral released Mistral Large 4 (Le Chonk), a 1-trillion-parameter multimodal model, now available with improved coding, cybersecurity, and reasoning efficiency. It leads open-weight benchmarks in coding and defensive security while offering three reasoning modes for cost-performance balance. Fully open-weight and European-trained, it empowers sovereign AI with customizable, self-hosted deployment options. We release the weights of Le Chonk, advancing the frontier of open models in agentic coding and cybersecurity. Earlier this month, we released Mistral Large 4 (Le Chonk), our flagship 1-trillion-parameter, natively multimodal model, in public preview. The response to the preview has exceeded our expectations, and we&#39;re grateful to our developer community, early-access customers, and partners for their support, collaboration, and feedback. Since then, we&#39;ve put that feedback to work while continuing to scale reinforcement learning, driving massive improvements in coding and defensive cybersecurity . Gains continue to accelerate, with no signs of saturation. Today, we&#39;re releasing the weights of this improved Mistral Large 4. The new model also features substantial gains in reasoning efficiency that give developers greater performance while lowering inference costs. Mistral Large 4 ranks as the best open-weight model for coding and among the world&#39;s most capable open models overall. Trained from scratch on European infrastructure with clear lineage from inception to release, it gives organizations a trusted foundation for Sovereign AI Mistral is defining the sovereign AI category and leading the open frontier. We&#39;re committed to building the world&#39;s most capable models and releasing them openly, giving developers, enterprises, and institutions the power to own, customize, and control their AI. Built for the Builders. Mistral Large 4 entered public preview with strong results across leading coding benchmarks. Just three weeks of additional reinforcement learning have since propelled it to the forefront of open-weight coding, taking the lead on FrontierCode and Terminal-Bench while matching the best open-weight models on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index with substantially greater token efficiency. On Cognition&#39;s private FrontierCode 2.0 benchmark, which assesses end-to-end code quality and whether a repository maintainer would merge the agent&#39;s change, Mistral Large 4 leads with a score of 19.7%. Agentic webdev: with no human help, Mistral Large 4 builds Orbital Trace, a live 3D map of roughly 19,000 satellites and debris pieces orbiting Earth. It pulls real tracking data, writes the app, uses it in a browser to catch its own bugs and slowdowns, then fixes what an independent reviewer flags until all requirements pass. Quantum chemistry solver: Mistral Large 4 writes, from scratch, software that calculates where electrons sit in a molecule, and checks its answers against a standard chemistry tool. It then builds a 3D page around it: pick a molecule such as benzene or water, drag a slider to pull it apart, and watch the electrons regroup as the bond breaks. Built for Sovereignty. As organizations build AI around their knowledge and workflows, sovereignty means that they retain control of the models they depend on and the intelligence they create. With Mistral Large 4, that control begins with the model itself. Organizations have the ability to inspect, customize, and deploy the model on their own infrastructure. They can use and improve Mistral Large 4 without depending on Mistral to serve it through an API. Mistral Large 4 was developed and trained from scratch in Europe, giving organizations clarity about the origins of the technology they’re building on. Control also extends to how AI operates inside an organization. Organizations can customize Mistral Large 4 around their proprietary data with Mistral Forge , and they can build, govern, and improve the resulting agents and applications using Mistral Studio. Mistral’s Applied AI experts can work alongside customer teams to build and deploy these tailored systems. Through that work, teams understand the architecture and decisions behind what they’ve built, along with the methods used to train and operate it, so they can run and improve their AI independently.
