If someone knows the number of a friend or loved one, they can call almost anywhere in the world for 5 minutes for free. Or you can call it and someone walking past might pick it up. Call the phone Dial +1 (775) 557-4848 to try to talk to a random Burner. Or add Playa Phone as a contact to make it easier to call later.
