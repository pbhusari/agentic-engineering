NOV 2026 When I first started designing this site, I would constantly look around my home for objects that I had never given a second thought. Two products stood out to me as wonderfully mechanical and common: the mechanical pencil and the spray bottle. The modern spray bottle emerged in the early 1970s, and by now there must be hundreds, maybe even thousands, of spray bottle designs out there in the world, with variations on their nozzle design, trigger mechanism and more. But they all utilize the same basic one-way-valve mechanism. The main question we want to answer: How does fluid keep spraying out, pull after pull, every time you squeeze the Trigger? Let's get into it! How Do You Use A Spray Bottle? Before we look inside, let’s cover the basics: pull the Trigger to spray, release it to reload.
