Based on Qwen3.8 27B, it retains the previous release’s 5.9 GB model footprint while significantly improving the capabilities developers need to build agents that work with private files and tools on-device. The result is by far the strongest AI model of its memory footprint for agentic workloads. With Ternary Bonsai 2 27B , we brought stronger reasoning, visual understanding, and tool use to local devices. Since then, we’ve focused on making the model more effective across long sequences of tool calls: interpreting results, adjusting when a step fails, and maintaining the task’s objective through the whole interaction. This release also continues our work to make Bonsai more practical by addressing excessive thinking and repetitive loops in the previous model. Compared with Ternary Bonsai 2, Ternary Bonsai 2.5 completes tasks in fewer turns, uses fewer tokens, and delivers results faster and more accurately. Agentic workflows are particularly sensitive to model degradation: small errors can compound across successive tool calls and derail an entire task. Ternary Bonsai 2.5 significantly narrows the performance gap to the full-precision base model while retaining its compact footprint. Preserving this behavior through longer tasks is an important milestone in improving the intelligence density of models. We evaluated Ternary Bonsai 2.5 27B against both Ternary Bonsai 2 27B and the full-precision Qwen 3.8 27B base model, as reported in the full whitepaper . We see marked improvements in agentic execution, coding, and multi-turn tool use, as shown in the table below. Even on a consumer-grade RTX 4090, it delivers 204 tokens/s with MTP and a prompt processing speed of approximately 3,060 tokens/s.
