" let us curate art on your walls, get paid if someone buys it. " and it worked. sort of. finding artists was easy, building the platform was easy. getting permission from venues was slow but straightforward. the problem was nobody actually bought the art. i remember the admin email notification that first time a piece was sold. it was a small painting of Janelle Monáe. the buyer was my friend, a gift to me. here it is, still in my possession. anyway ArtSpot failed. five years later i paid back my investors (+ 13% interest). but the vision never went away. i love making and consuming art. opinionated platform i probably say " the most popular feature request since launch is ______ " too often. after saying no (and yes) to so many requests, it's tough to keep count. but with certainty, one of our strongest convictions was about color. TRMNL is not a television. it is not a clock. we avoid trying to do too much. then grayscale happened . photos look great. art is possible. community member Clark debuted Jaws (yes the movie), 1 frame at a time . 1-bit Jaws on TRMNL which got us thinking... maybe we were wrong about color. maybe it aligns with our vision and just maybe, it makes it even bigger. introducing Frame today i'm thrilled to bury the lede and announce Frame . it's a 31.5 inch full color ePaper display, enclosed in a hand made wooden body that we manufacture ourselves. yes, we opened our own woodworking shop a few miles from HQ. a closer look at the workshop is coming soon. Schedule + Weather + Art in 1 screen Frame gets all the same features as other TRMNL devices, with a few additional perks spanning both the web platform and the hardware itself. watch the short film or read along below. Clock Sync often requested, always ignored, until now. Frame will update at specified moments of the day versus a relative "every N minutes" cadence. this reduces screen flicker while also extending the battery life and adding continuity to your schedule. Offline Mode the best interface is no interface the internet is cool but totally unnecessary. Frame's microSD port lets you run devices completely offline with zero personal information reaching our servers. Dedicated Power (optional) 1st port is available on the side panel like all TRMNL devices, a rechargeable battery is included (10-20k mAh available). but with Frame we added an extra USB-C port on the rear of the device, accessible with a small hole through the back panel for always-on connectivity. Color Expertise Lazarus workbench some of my colleagues are snobs about ePaper dithering algorithms and LUTs (look-up tables). this is now your gain. over the past few months we've been developing Lazarus, a color conversion engine that we think provides Frame the best color matching on the market for any Spectra 6 display. it's also coming soon to our 4-color device , and yes it will be open source for your own tuning pleasure. Three Orientations Frame at Cafe Brûlée in LaGrange, Georgia Hang Frame in portrait or landscape orientation without additional hardware, both are built-in and flush mounted. You can optionally buy or build your own kickstand. And More we're negotiating with some of our favorite community developers to build more Frame-centric features. paying homage to ArtSpot , we just created a Local Art plugin where anyone can submit their work and even sell it through the TRMNL platform. transparent supply chain before TRMNL i spent 10 years in b2b software and knew nothing about making physical products for everyday people. nobody ever blasted me on reddit about injection mold flow lines or damaged UPS packages. at best i'd get a G2 review every 3 months. this all changed in July 2024 and after shipping 60,000 devices to 79 countries we've gotten pickier about operational design. chief among these new requirements is doing as much production as possible in-house. here are Frame's components and where they're sourced: Component Location Lead Times Inventory EPD (31.5" screen) Overseas 2-3 months 300 in warehouse, 200 en route Packaging Alabama, USA 1-2 months samples received, design tweaks sent for larger batch Wooden body TRMNL shop, Pine Mountain, Georgia 1 week made-to-order pending wood species chosen at checkout USB-C Charging Cable and Block Overseas 3-4 weeks 500 in warehouse, includes American + European style adapters Plastic parts TRMNL HQ, LaGrange, Georgia 1-2 days made daily from a growing fleet of 3D printers PCBA Overseas 2-3 weeks 1,000 full assemblies (raw components) in warehouse, bulk pick &amp;
