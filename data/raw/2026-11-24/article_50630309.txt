It comes nearly five months after Tidal said it would demonetize any AI-powered song, force those artists to label their tracks as either AI-generated or AI-assisted and ban AI songs that were associated with &#x201C;fraudulent activity,&#x201D; a category that includes songs impersonating established artists and efforts to &#x201C;deceive listeners.&#x201D; Related Stories &#039;Russell Brand: Open Secret&#039; Director Says Finding Contributors Wasn&#039;t Easy: &#039;There&#039;s Been a Landscape of Fear&#039; Netflix Orders Harlan Coben Limited Series &#039;The Innocent&#039; From &#039;I Will Find You&#039; Team &#x201C;But that&#x2019;s enough, as 43% of tracks distributed to us in the last 90 days have been detected as AI-generated. Something more drastic needs to be done,&#x201D;&#xA0;wrote Robert Andersen, the Tidal Lead at parent company Block, in an X thread on Tuesday. &#x201C;We&#x2019;ll begin removing all wholly AI-generated tracks from our catalog, and you&#x2019;ll see them completely gone by the beginning of the year.&#x201D; Andersen said that 96% of all AI-generated songs receive zero plays. The move will cleave the platform of 29 million tracks, he added, and have it reject between 80,000 to 85,000 songs a day. &#x201C;We encourage our peers to follow suit,&#x201D;&#xA0;he wrote. The move comes as competitors such as Spotify and Apple Music continue to test approaches to combat the rise of AI-generated tracks proliferating on their platforms, many of which the services have labeled some version of &#x201C;slop&#x201D; or &#x201C;spam.&#x201D; Apple Music said it would also begin labeling AI-generated or AI-assisted tracks on its platform, while Spotify said it would label artists it deemed AI creations as an &#x201C;AI Persona.&#x201D; Spotify said in a blog post last month it also removed 50 million slop or spam tracks from users&#8217; recommendations this year, which came after it removed 75 million of those songs from its catalog entirely. The revelation came among a number of updates regarding Spotify&#8217;s efforts to combat AI spam. &#x201C;This work isn&#x2019;t finished,&#x201D; Spotify added. &#x201C;AI moves fast, and bad actors adapt just as fast. But as AI makes music more abundant, we believe listeners will value human artistry even more.
