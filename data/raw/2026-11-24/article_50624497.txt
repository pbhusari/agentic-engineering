I remember watching the talk The Birth &amp; Death of JavaScript by Gary Bernhardt and falling in love with the whimsical yet dreadfully serious unfolding of the story of how JavaScript will come to dominate all software. Others have noted the inevitability of JavaScript’s dominance, so perhaps it was not surprising that Atwood’s Law holds true. Atwood’s Law Any application that can be written in JavaScript, will eventually be written in JavaScript. — Jeff Atwood, 2009 I highly recommend watching the talk if you have not already seen it, but the short and skinny is a lookback from 2035 and how JavaScript came to be the defacto language for all software. In Bernhardt’s retelling of events, the date is worked back from 2035, however unsurprisingly, large language models have rapidly sped up the timeline.
