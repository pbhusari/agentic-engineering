Ladybird is now in alpha - Ladybird Download News Get involved Sponsors FAQ Donate Andreas Kling &mdash; Mon, 23 Nov 2026 Ladybird is now in alpha Hello friends! Ladybird is now in alpha. You can download it for macOS and Linux. I’ve been looking forward to writing this post for a long time. :^) Ladybird is a browser with a new engine. The engine is our own, built from the ground up, and not a fork. The browser around it is modest, but functional. This is an alpha, so some websites won’t work, and it will crash sometimes. When that happens, tell us! You can report a broken site from inside the browser. After a crash, you can read the crash report and decide whether to send it. Our privacy policy has the details. We want security nerds to hack it! A new browser gets hacked, over and over, and that’s part of the process, so we make no promises about security today. When you pwn us, please tell us how so we can harden Ladybird! The alpha is a rolling channel, and we’ll be pushing out automatic updates regularly. This is the part I’m most excited about. Ladybird improves quickly, and until now you had to build it from source to see that. If you build websites, try yours in Ladybird and tell us what breaks. It supports WebDriver for automated testing, as well as the Firefox DevTools protocol. Thank you to our sponsors and donors, and to everyone who has tested, reported bugs, and written code over the years. There’s still a mountain of work ahead of us, but I’m very happy that you can finally try Ladybird for yourself! Stay in the loop Monthly updates about Ladybird, straight to your inbox.
