Farewell #601 Copy link Copy link Backlog Backlog Farewell #601 Copy link Description p-e-w opened on Nov 28, 2026 Issue body actions Dear friends of Heretic, as foreshadowed a while ago , my journey has now come to an end. Unfortunately, this includes my work on Heretic, which has meant so much to me. I sincerely regret the inconvenience this will cause the community. I am archiving this repository to preserve it in its current, high-quality state, and to prevent issue and pull request spam from turning it into a trash pile. I encourage interested contributors to organize on Discord, Matrix, or elsewhere, and decide whether and how to continue the project without me. I am deliberately not naming a successor, nor am I suggesting any particular infrastructure. The future of Heretic is now in the hands of all of you. As AI continues to spread through your lives, you will find that controlling the models you use gradually transforms from an issue of Free Speech into an issue of Free Thought. AI will be the lens through which you see the world, not just a tool for getting things done. The fight for the ability to shape that lens yourself is nothing less than the fight for the humanity in you. Farewell, my friends. In times like these, one should be proud to be labeled a heretic.
